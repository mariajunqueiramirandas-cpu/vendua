## Intent

A 15-second product teaser for Venduá to post on social media and Instagram stories. Pure brand
sting: mostly typography and Duá, one line of what it is, and "Em breve". Motion is the message.

## Customizations

- Music and sound effects are synthesized for this video (no library tracks), cut on one beat
  grid so every hit lands on a visual event — "really cool sound effects and music, synced perfectly".
- No voiceover; on-screen text carries the words (stories often play muted).

## Notes

- Launch rules from `site/README.md`: the only call to action is "Em breve"; no prices, no sign-up
  or contact CTA; features only from the planned list; no "sob medida", "sem taxa", "grátis".
- Duá is never mirrored, recolored or filtered. No photos, people, AI images or fake numbers.
- Brand: green #123C32, lime #D9F875, cream #F7F4EA; Space Grotesk (display), Figtree (body).
- Instagram stories: keep text out of the top ~250 px and bottom ~340 px (UI overlays).
