# ADR 0035: The PDV — counter sales, mesas and the caixa

- Status: Accepted (implemented 2026-10-06, Core migration 0097)
- Date: 2026-10-06

## Context

Every primary food competitor sells a PDV, built in or integrated
([`competitor-parity.md`](../competitor-parity.md), P-023), and most of them also take dine-in
orders on a table (P-015). Venduá only took orders from the storefront and the Vendedor, both
delivery or pickup. A store that also serves a counter or a salão ran a second system next to
ours, so its menu, stock and numbers lived in two places. Domínio's §2.3 (PDV), §2.4 (Caixa) and
§2.5 (Mesas) list what merchants expect: open the caixa with a float, ring up a sale with mixed
payments and change, sangria and suprimento, close by counting each method, comandas on tables
that collect rounds and split the bill.

The owner's scope call (2026-10-06):

- First release: counter sales, the caixa and mesas with comandas. This answers "do we serve
  dine-in?" with yes, for orders the staff take. Shopper-side QR table ordering stays P-015.
- No NFC-e (P-004) and no card-machine integration (TEF, Mercado Pago Point). A card payment is
  recorded as crédito or débito on the store's own machine. ADR 0027's "no Smart POS terminals"
  still holds.
- Plans: Bandeira and Pangolim. Mirim does not include it.

## Decision

**One more admin area, not another app.** `/admin/pdv` is in the merchant admin, like the
kitchen (ADR 0029). It has three screens: Vender (the counter), Mesas (tables and their
comandas) and Caixa. It uses the same session, live stream and offline queue. Any role sells,
runs comandas and runs the caixa. Discounts, voiding a payment, cancelling a comanda, the tables
and the service-charge setting are for managers.

**Every sale is an order on the existing path.** A counter sale and each round of a comanda is a
row in `orders` with `source = 'pdv'`. Its number comes from the same sequence under the same
advisory lock as checkout, and it is born `placed` and moved to `confirmed` in the same
transaction through `transitionOrder`. Pedidos, the kitchen display, printing and the reports
therefore see it with no special case. `delivery.mode` gains `dine_in`, with `delivery.table`
holding the table's label. A dine-in order never goes out for delivery. "Para levar" is a
`pickup` order. "Entregar agora" walks a sale straight to `delivered` for things nobody prepares.

A PDV order has no cart: migration 0097 makes `orders.cart_id` nullable, with a check that only
`source = 'pdv'` may leave it empty. No shopper can reach it through the storefront, and it sends
no WhatsApp step messages (the shopper never saw a checkout). It still gives loyalty stamps when
the staff typed the customer's phone.

**Money is computed in Core, as everywhere.** The counter keeps only what was picked (product,
quantity, options, kit picks, note). `POST /pdv/quote` prices it with the storefront's own line
pricing (`priceLine`), and a sale re-prices it under the product locks and refuses a total that
moved since the quote. Discounts (fixed or percent, with a reason) and the comanda's service
charge are computed in Core, and so is the split of a bill into N shares and the change of a
cash payment. The storefront's discount or surcharge by payment method does not apply at the counter.

**The caixa is a session with its own ledger.** Migration 0097 adds:

- `pdv_tables`: the store's tables, each a label and a sort position. Archived, never deleted.
- `pdv_tabs`: a comanda, on a table or only labelled ("Comanda 12"). Its rounds are the orders
  with its `tab_id`. It holds the service-charge rate snapshotted at opening, whether the
  customer took it, and a manager's discount. At most one open comanda per table.
- `cash_sessions`: a caixa, opened with a float and closed with what was counted per method.
  At most one open per store.
- `cash_movements`: sangria (money out) and suprimento (money in), each with a reason and who.
- `pdv_payments`: every payment taken at the counter: the method (dinheiro, Pix, crédito,
  débito, vale-refeição), the amount applied to the bill, what was handed over and the change,
  the caixa it went into, and the order or comanda it paid. A payment can be voided (manager,
  with a reason) while its comanda is still open.
- `store_settings.pdv_service_bps`: the service charge ("10% do garçom"), 0 to 20%.

No payment is taken without an open caixa. A counter sale is paid in full when it is made. A
comanda takes partial payments until nothing remains, then closes on its own: its orders are
marked paid and walked to `delivered`, and the table frees. An order from the storefront that
is paid at the counter (the kitchen's ready rail says when to charge) is received through the
same payments, so its money lands in the caixa too.

Closing the caixa records the count for each method next to what Core expected (float plus
cash taken plus suprimentos minus sangrias, and the sums of the other methods) and keeps the
difference. Attendants close blind: only managers see the expected amounts before closing.

**Plan gate.** `pdv` is a new plan feature (Bandeira and Pangolim, and pilot stores). Every
`/admin/v1/pdv` route checks it, and the nav shows the lock like the kitchen's.

All new tables have `tenant_id` with the RLS policies; every mutation goes through the
Idempotency-Key claim, writes `audit_log` and emits the new `pdv` admin topic. The routes and
shapes are in [`features/pdv.md`](../features/pdv.md).

## Consequences

- Dine-in exists for orders the staff take. The kitchen ticket and the printed comanda show the
  table. Shopper-side table ordering (P-015's QR part) can now build on `dine_in` and `pdv_tabs`.
- Revenue reports include counter sales. The service charge is not order revenue: it lives on
  the comanda and in the caixa's closing report.
- Phone orders for delivery are not in the counter yet; staff use the storefront or the
  Vendedor for those. Printing the bill ("conferência") and the caixa report on the thermal
  printer are next.
- NFC-e and card-machine integration remain separate projects (P-004 and a fresh research
  round on Point, Stone and PagBank).

## Alternatives considered

- **A server-side cart per sale**, reusing `placeOrderTx`. It would have brought delivery for
  free, but checkout's rules (store open, minimum order, the shopper's name and phone, payment
  adjustments) are the shopper's and would each need a staff exception, and every tap would be
  a write. The stateless quote keeps the counter fast and the order path unchanged.
- **One order per comanda**, edited as rounds arrive. Orders are immutable once placed; the
  kitchen, printing and stock all assume that. A round is an order.
