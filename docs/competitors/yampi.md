# Yampi

> Role: Secondary (generalist storefront) · Last researched: 2026-10-01 · Confidence: Medium

Checkout-conversion focused; the only generalist here with accounts and cashback documented.

## Distinctive features

- Customer area (accounts) that can be enabled.
- Cashback credit wallet.
- Order bump, upsell and a hosted checkout focus.
- Many ad pixels (Meta, GA4, Google Ads, TikTok, Pinterest, Kwai) and a documented Meta CAPI guide.
- Subscription support; multi-currency (Real or Dollar).
- Yampi Pay, reseller affiliate program.
