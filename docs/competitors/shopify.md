# Shopify

> Role: Secondary (global reference) · Last researched: 2026-10-01 · Confidence: Medium (official help pages; Brazil specifics third-party)

The global reference, but weakly localized for Brazil, which is where it differs from the others.

## Distinctive features

- Shop Pay accelerated checkout; passwordless customer accounts.
- Markets for multi-region, multi-currency selling.
- Free first-party Subscriptions app; gift cards built in.
- Email and SMS marketing, Collabs creator partnerships.
- Not available in Brazil: Shopify Payments. Pix needs a third-party gateway (Mercado Pago, Pagar.me, Appmax).
- NF-e is not native; Brazilian stores use apps or ERPs (Bling, Tiny, Spedy).
