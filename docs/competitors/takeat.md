# Takeat

> Role: Primary (cardápio digital) · Last researched: 2026-10-01 · Confidence: Medium

Importable today ([menu-import §3](../menu-import.md#3-platforms): easy; filter dine-in-only data). Leans restaurant ERP rather than storefront marketing.

## Distinctive features

- Own delivery channel with no per-order commission as the pitch.
- Restaurant finance: DRE, CMV, multi-location dashboard, accountant reports.
- Fiscal PDV across tax regimes (Simples, Presumido, Real).
- KDS by station, kiosk, tablet and digital waiter.
- AI assistant "Teka" for WhatsApp; automated coupons and cashback promotions.
- Syncs iFood, 99Food and Keeta.

## Common to the set

Storefront, order management, PDV, QR table ordering, delivery tooling, coupons, loyalty, iFood sync and AI appear at most primary competitors. The list above is what sets Takeat apart.
