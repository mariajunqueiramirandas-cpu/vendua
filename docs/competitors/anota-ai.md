# anota.ai

> Role: Primary (cardápio digital) · Last researched: 2026-10-01 · Confidence: Medium (own site fetched, no deep pages)

Broadest platform of the food set. Importing its menus is blocked from our test network ([menu-import §3](../menu-import.md#3-platforms)).

## Distinctive features

- AI attendant on WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram, text and audio, 24/7.
- Abandoned-cart recovery and a WhatsApp campaign sender.
- Loyalty with points and cashback, coupons, Google Offers.
- Built-in PDV, cash register, KDS, waiter app with digital comanda.
- NF-e issuing and pixel tracking.
- Delivery control with tracking and driver management.
