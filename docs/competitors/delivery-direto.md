# Delivery Direto

> Role: Primary (cardápio digital) · Last researched: 2026-10-01 · Confidence: Medium

Importable today ([menu-import §3](../menu-import.md#3-platforms): easy). Pitched at brands with several stores.

## Distinctive features

- "Recuperador de Pedidos": abandoned-order recovery.
- Custom domain.
- One dashboard for WhatsApp, web and QR orders, with a WhatsApp bot.
- Recurring-sales loyalty (automatic).
- Integrations with third-party POS (Saipos, Colibri, Totvs Chef) instead of its own PDV.

## Common to the set

Storefront, order management, PDV, QR table ordering, delivery tooling, coupons, loyalty, iFood sync and AI appear at most primary competitors. The list above is what sets Delivery Direto apart.
