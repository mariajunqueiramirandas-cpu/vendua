# Nuvemshop

> Role: Secondary (generalist storefront) · Last researched: 2026-10-01 · Confidence: Medium–high (official help pages)

Closest generalist peer in Brazil by feature breadth.

## Distinctive features

- Native subscription sales (higher-tier plan) and a pt/es/en language and currency setting.
- Nuvem Envio shipping, NF-e from the panel, Nuvem Pago.
- Meta pixel and Conversions API; Instagram Shopping and Meta catalog sync; WhatsApp channel.
- Marketplaces (Mercado Livre, Amazon, Shopee) and its own PDV.
- Automatic sitemap, 301 redirects, AI SEO.
