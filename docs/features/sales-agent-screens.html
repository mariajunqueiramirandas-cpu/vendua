Vendedor Screens

Venduá admin · design proposal

Vendedor, a loja que vende sozinha

The merchant-facing screens for the store's AI seller, building on the first board by the team. They are built from the merchant admin's own tokens (Creme and Noite, forest and spark, Space Grotesk, Figtree and Instrument Serif) and share nothing with the CRM agent console. All data is sample data for a fictional pizzeria, Forno da Vila, whose Vendedor the owner named Ana.

Three voices

Shoppers on paper, the Vendedor on spark, the owner in forest. Who said what reads at a glance, in both themes, without relying on colour alone: every Vendedor run is signed.

Money looks like a receipt

Totals, fees and Pix codes come from Core and render as receipt paper with a dashed tear. The Vendedor's own words never carry a figure, so the guarantee is visible.

The floor is always visible

Every conversation says who is answering. Taking over is one tap here, or simply replying from the phone. Handing back is one tap too.

Proof before trust

Ensaio and Cliente oculto show what it would do on this store's own menu before it talks to anyone. "Por quê" sits under every action.

Phone, 375 px

The owner runs the store from the phone. These nine screens cover the first day and every day after: meet it, watch it sell, step in, get called, teach it, rehearse it, check it, see what it earned, set it up.

Desktop, 1240 px

Conversas as a three-pane inbox: who needs you, the thread with its live sacola, and the customer with "por quê" for the selected message. Numbers that don't look like customers (suppliers, couriers, family) wait under "outros", held and unanswered. Three keys cover the day at the counter.

Deliberately absent

What these screens leave out on purpose, most of it straight from the CRM agent console.

An autonomy sliderOff, copilot, supervised and autopilot answer "may it send?". A shopper can't wait for approval, so the question here is "when does it answer?", in four plain modes.
A queue of drafts to approveEnsaio replaces it: the Vendedor drafts silently while the owner keeps answering, and the agreement score earns the switch to live.
Run timelines and raw JSONThe owner sees plain receipts and "por quê". Spans, tokens, cache hits and cost stay in the staff console, per store.
Dense neutral chromeNo Inter at 14 px or 32 px controls. Cream paper, 48 px targets, display numerals and one-handed reach, like the rest of the admin.
Sparkles everywhereSpark marks only what is alive: Ana's ring while she's on, the newest sale, a conversation happening now. Being AI is not decorated with glitter, gradients or magic wands.
A confidence percentageOwners get receipts and "por quê", not model scores. "87% de certeza" invites a guess about what it means; a plain reason can be checked against the store.

Sample data only: Forno da Vila, Ana, Carla and every figure are fictional.
